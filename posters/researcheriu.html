HCI Master's Program • 2024

Research Assistant

Worked across two research labs focusing on interactive data visualization with museum field studies, AI-assisted qualitative analysis using Conceptual Metaphor Theory, and human-centered AI research with older adults.

Primary Investigators: Francesco Cafaro, Aquesha Martin-Hammond

Lab 1: Data Viz & Museum Field Studies

  • → Goal: Evaluate interactive data visualizations for museum deployment.
  • → Methods: Deployed at Indiana State Museum; conducted field studies utilizing Kinect sensors to capture children's movement. Designed artifacts using Figma & Premiere Pro.
  • → Findings: Movement-based metaphors supported learning. Kinect effectively captured reflections during interactions.

Lab 1: AI & Conceptual Metaphor Theory (CMT)

  • → Goal: Investigate if LLMs can reliably assist in qualitative analysis using CMT.
  • → Methods: Collected 5,000+ Reddit posts via Python/API. Iteratively refined prompts using Gemini and NotebookLM with curated schemas.
  • → Findings: High-confidence outputs aligned with experts; medium aligned with students. Full consistency elusive due to LLM variability.

Lab 2: Human-Centered AI for Older Adults

  • → Goal: Examine older adults' perceptions of AI systems for health information seeking.
  • → Methods: Prototyped AI artifacts. Conducted 20 usability testing sessions and scenario-based task surveys focusing on trust.
  • → Findings: Privacy and data safety were primary concerns. Transparency about AI limitations significantly increased trust.

Impact & Cross-Lab Synthesis

  • ★ Supported real-world deployment and evaluation of interactive museum installations.
  • ★ Demonstrated how AI tools can augment—but not replace—human qualitative research.
  • ★ Concluded that embodied cognition principles apply across both physical (museum) and digital (AI) interactions.
