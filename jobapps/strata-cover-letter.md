On AI in research workflows: "interest in incorporating AI tools while maintaining research quality and rigor" is the exact tension I've spent the last year and a half studying. In Dr. Aquesha Martin-Hammond's lab I led an AI-assisted qualitative analysis study applying Conceptual Metaphor Theory across 5,000+ Reddit posts using NotebookLM and Gemini. The headline finding wasn't "AI can do qualitative coding" — it was that confidence-band reliability mattered more than the output itself: high-confidence interpretations consistently matched expert human judgment, medium-confidence outputs matched what a trained student researcher would produce, and low-confidence outputs were frequently nonsense. That gave me a practical framework for when AI augments synthesis and analysis credibly and when it just appears to — which is the question any team incorporating AI into research has to answer well to avoid quietly losing rigor.
