# Nuuly — Customer Insights Analyst

**Application question:** *Please share why you would be a unique addition to the brand or this specific role?*

---

What makes me unique to Nuuly is that I've already spent five-plus years inside the same shift in fashion economics Nuuly is built on. Since 2020 I've worked as a textile wholesaler, sourcing and curating American-made vintage clothing for second-hand boutiques across the U.S. — handling, evaluating, and pricing on the second-life side of fashion at the garment level. Nuuly's whole model rests on the same logic: clothes have more than one useful life if they're chosen well, and I've made my living deciding which ones. Since 2019 I've also worked as a Visual Director and freelance photographer for fashion and lifestyle brands, so the visual register a fashion brand reads in is a working tool for me, not an abstract preference.

The role's craft sits naturally on top of that. My MS in Human-Computer Interaction focuses on accessible design and UX research — which maps unusually well to Nuuly's inclusive sizing range (XXS–5X, adaptive, maternity), where the customer is intentionally a wider population than most apparel brands plan around. That research craft has come through three lenses over the last two-plus years. As Lead Product Researcher at Darzy.ai, I built research operations from scratch — recruitment pipelines, interview protocols, and a shared insight repository — for a remote-first startup with no prior infrastructure, which is essentially the maintain-and-expand task this role describes. As a Research Assistant across two HCI labs at Indiana University Indianapolis, I led an AI-assisted qualitative analysis of 5,000+ Reddit posts using NotebookLM and Gemini, and ran 20 moderated usability sessions with older adults on an AI-driven health tool. And I currently lead my graduate capstone as Lead Product Designer with TruePulse Health, running mixed-methods research with women across the pregnancy continuum — a sensitive customer group held to the same rigor as everything else. The qual-plus-quant rhythm this role asks for is the rhythm of my actual week.

What I'd bring that's harder to hire for: an insights analyst who genuinely understands the merchandise, the customer's relationship to second-life clothing, and how a fashion brand reads when it's working — alongside the data and methods, not separate from them.
