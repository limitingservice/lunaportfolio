On the AI-decision-intelligence side, my research has been pointed at exactly the trust-and-validation question Accrete's Expert Agents have to answer. As a Research Assistant in Dr. Aquesha Martin-Hammond's lab at Indiana University Indianapolis, I led an AI-assisted qualitative analysis study applying Conceptual Metaphor Theory across 5,000+ Reddit posts using NotebookLM and Gemini. The finding wasn't "AI can do qualitative coding" — it was that confidence-band reliability mattered more than the output itself: high-confidence interpretations consistently matched expert human judgment; medium-confidence outputs matched what a trained student researcher would produce; low-confidence outputs were frequently nonsense. That gave me a working framework for when AI augments expert decision-making versus when it just pretends to — which is the question any team designing AI agents for "high-stakes decisions" has to answer well.

Different domains, same recurring question: how do you design an AI-driven system that domain experts actually trust enough to act on?
