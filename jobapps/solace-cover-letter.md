On structured evidence work specifically: as a Research Assistant at IU Indianapolis, I led an AI-assisted qualitative analysis study applying Conceptual Metaphor Theory across 5,000+ Reddit posts using NotebookLM and Gemini. The methodological finding wasn't "AI can do qualitative coding" — it was that confidence-band reliability matters more than the output itself. High-confidence interpretations consistently matched expert human judgment; medium-confidence outputs matched what a trained student researcher would produce; low-confidence outputs were nonsense more often than not. That's a structured, defensible framework for adopting AI-assisted literature research tools without overpromising what they can do — which is the exact "willingness to evaluate and adopt AI-assisted literature research tools" the role description names, paired with the "high standards for defensible research and willingness to be honest about what the data can and cannot support" you're asking for.
