Dear Puffco Team,

The most directly relevant project I have for this role is also my most recent: a browser-based AR virtual try-on for eyewear that I designed and built end-to-end as a study in e-commerce UX. The whole project started from a single statistic — online eyewear has a 30–50% return rate driven primarily by "didn't look right on me" — and tracks the funnel logic from there: native AR apps lose ~88% of interested users between "Click Try On" and "Actually try on," browser-based AR keeps conversion 7× higher by skipping the app store detour, and pre-prompting for camera access lifts permission acceptance from ~40% to ~85%. Every screen — the PDP, the consent flow, the face-acquisition state, the live try-on with inline color switching — was designed against a specific funnel drop-off point. The full case study is at fernandojluna.com.

That's the closest thing in my portfolio to the work in the JD: data-driven, conversion-focused product design where every decision has to defend itself against a metric.
